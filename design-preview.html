Below are five directions built with the real content: six guest covers, Sam and Tom cutouts, the show logo, and the site's DM Sans / DM Mono type plus brand greens and teals. Hover every card; each concept has a hover state worth watching. Use the toggle top-right for light mode.

01 · Episode crossfade

your cycle-animation idea

A tall media panel cycles through all six guest covers on a slow crossfade loop with a subtle zoom drift. A mono chip in the corner names the guest on screen. Calm and editorial, zero JS.

03 · Guest deck fan

tactile, physical metaphor

04 · Filmstrip ticker

ambient motion, pauses on hover

All six covers run in an infinite marquee across the card's top edge, faded at both ends, under a pulsing "On air" pill. The strip pauses when you hover so you can actually look. Motion says "this show keeps producing".
